# Coverage Gaps

## Eligibility
* **NOS**: Covered. Defined in the guidelines and FAQ (maximum age limits, family income under Rs. 6 lakh, 55% minimum marks, restricted to one child per family).
* **NFST**: **Covered.** Per `nfst_part_a_full_guidelines_2021-26.pdf`: eligibility is completion of PG/Master's with minimum 55% marks; admission to M.Phil/M.Phil+Ph.D/Ph.D in an eligible institution category (UGC 2(f)/12(B), deemed university, government-grant institution, or Institute of National Importance); maximum age 36 years as on 1 July of the award year; NO income criteria for this component (income criteria applies only to Part-B, National Scholarship).

## Documents Required
* **NOS**: Covered.
* **NFST**: **Covered.** Section 3.3 of the same document lists: passport photo, ST/PVTG certificate, 10th/matriculation certificate (DOB proof), Divyangjan certificate (if applicable), PG marksheet with CGPA-to-percentage conversion formula (if applicable), admission/joining certificate for M.Phil/Ph.D, and offer letter (for IIT/AIIMS/IIM/IISER admits). The verification manual's document list is now confirmed consistent with this.

## Slot/Quota Rules
* **NOS**: Covered.
* **NFST**: **Covered.** Total 750 fellowships/year, unfilled slots carried forward to next year, no state/university-wise ceiling. Priority cascade: (1) Divyangjan — 38 slots (5% of 750), (2) PVTG — 25 slots, (3) Female — 225 slots (30% of 750, INCLUDES female Divyangjan/PVTG candidates — overlapping, not exclusive), (4) ST Others — 462 slots. Unfilled slots in a higher-priority bucket cascade down to the next bucket in order. IIT/AIIMS/IIM/IISER offer-holders get priority, with ST Others reduced proportionately to accommodate them.

## Scoring/Merit
* **NOS**: Covered.
* **NFST**: **Mostly covered, one flag remains.** Base guideline (Section 3.5) states merit is based on PG examination marks. This is SUPERSEDED for the 2025-26 cycle specifically by the notice dated 04.08.2026: 50% weightage to UGC-NET/CSIR-NET score + 50% weightage to Master's (or 4-year Bachelor's with Research, NEP 2020) marks, both scaled to 100. **Remaining gap:** the exact method for scaling/normalizing NET scores to 100, and how candidates without a NET score are scored, are not stated anywhere — keep these two specific fields as verified:false.

## Lifecycle Stages
* **NOS**: Covered.
* **NFST**: **Covered.** Full lifecycle now traceable: online application (portal opens ~1 July, closes ~30 September) → institute-level document verification → Ministry-level verification (defective applications sent back to student → resubmit at institute → re-verify → resubmit to Ministry) → merit list preparation by Selection Committee (Secretary MoTA as chair, JS&FA, JS Scholarship Division, Director/Deputy Secretary Scholarship Division, a Social Justice Ministry representative, a UGC representative) → provisional merit list published → provisional award letter issued → scholar must submit joining report within 1 month or award is cancelled and a waitlisted candidate is considered → quarterly continuation certificates (due 10 Jul / 10 Oct / 10 Jan / 10 Apr) → HRA marking → yearly progress report → thesis upload gates final quarter's payment → course completion certificate submitted.

## Rates/Amounts
* **NOS**: Covered.
* **NFST**: **Mostly covered, one flag remains.** Base guideline rates (effective 1 April 2022): M.Phil — ₹31,000/month (both streams) + ₹10,000/year contingency (Humanities) or ₹12,000/year (Science/Engineering); Ph.D — ₹31,000/month for first 2 years, ₹35,000/month for remaining 3 years + ₹20,500/year contingency (Humanities) or ₹25,000/year (Science/Engineering). HRA = UGC rates (8%/16%/24% by city category). Escort allowance for Divyangjan = ₹2,000/month. **Remaining gap:** a later rate-revision notice (referenced on the Ministry site as "Revised rate of fellowship w.e.f 1.1.2023") is not in your documents/ folder and likely raises these figures (a real award letter elsewhere shows ₹37,000/₹42,000) — mark current-year exact rates as verified:false and use the ₹31,000/₹35,000 figures as the dated fallback until that notice is located.

## Post-Selection Rules
* **NOS**: Covered.
* **NFST**: **Covered.** Tenure: 2 years (M.Phil), 5 years (M.Phil+Ph.D or Ph.D). Leave policy: 30 days/year plus public holidays; maternity/paternity leave per Govt norms with fellowship extended by the leave period; "intermittent break" up to 1 year total, usable up to 3 times, for women scholars; academic leave without fellowship permissible once for max 1 year. University transfer and subject/course change permitted with prior institute approval. Discontinuation requires a submitted undertaking; refund obligations apply if scholarship was already availed elsewhere. Cancellation applies on proven misconduct or subsequent ineligibility. Note: this document does not describe a formal "JRF to SRF upgrade" process by that name — NFST's M.Phil-to-Ph.D case is instead handled as "continuing candidate, no fresh application needed, max 5 years total including M.Phil period" (Section 5.4.2). Treat "JRF/SRF" terminology as not applicable to NFST; use "M.Phil-to-Ph.D continuation" instead.
