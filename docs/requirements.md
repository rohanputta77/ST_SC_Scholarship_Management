# Requirements

## Eligibility

### NFST
* **NFST-E-01**: Candidate must have completed PG/Master's with a minimum of 55% marks. (Source: `nfst_part_a_full_guidelines_2021-26.pdf`)
* **NFST-E-02**: Candidate must be admitted to an M.Phil, M.Phil+Ph.D, or Ph.D program in an eligible institution (UGC 2(f)/12(B), deemed university, government-grant institution, or Institute of National Importance). (Source: `nfst_part_a_full_guidelines_2021-26.pdf`)
* **NFST-E-03**: Maximum age limit is 36 years as on 1 July of the award year. (Source: `nfst_part_a_full_guidelines_2021-26.pdf`)
* **NFST-E-04**: No income criteria is applicable for the NFST component. (Source: `nfst_part_a_full_guidelines_2021-26.pdf`)

### NOS
* **NOS-E-01**: Candidate must meet maximum age limits. (Source: `RevisedGuidelinesNOS07102022.pdf` / `nos_faq_2025-26.pdf.pdf`)
* **NOS-E-02**: Family income must be under Rs. 6 lakh. (Source: `RevisedGuidelinesNOS07102022.pdf` / `nos_faq_2025-26.pdf.pdf`)
* **NOS-E-03**: Candidate must have a minimum of 55% marks in the qualifying exam. (Source: `RevisedGuidelinesNOS07102022.pdf` / `nos_faq_2025-26.pdf.pdf`)
* **NOS-E-04**: The scholarship is restricted to only one child per family. (Source: `RevisedGuidelinesNOS07102022.pdf` / `nos_faq_2025-26.pdf.pdf`)

## Documents

### NFST
* **NFST-D-01**: Applicant must provide a passport photo, ST/PVTG certificate, and 10th/matriculation certificate as DOB proof. (Source: `nfst_part_a_full_guidelines_2021-26.pdf`, Section 3.3)
* **NFST-D-02**: If applicable, candidate must provide a Divyangjan certificate. (Source: `nfst_part_a_full_guidelines_2021-26.pdf`, Section 3.3)
* **NFST-D-03**: Applicant must provide a PG marksheet with a CGPA-to-percentage conversion formula if applicable. (Source: `nfst_part_a_full_guidelines_2021-26.pdf`, Section 3.3)
* **NFST-D-04**: Applicant must provide an admission/joining certificate for M.Phil/Ph.D or an offer letter for IIT/AIIMS/IIM/IISER admits. (Source: `nfst_part_a_full_guidelines_2021-26.pdf`, Section 3.3)

### NOS
* **NOS-D-01**: Required documents include income certificate, ST certificate, offer of admission, and conversion factor formula. (Source: `RevisedGuidelinesNOS07102022.pdf` / `nos_faq_2025-26.pdf.pdf`)

## Slots / Quota Rules

### NFST
* **NFST-S-01**: The scheme awards a total of 750 fellowships per year. Unfilled slots are carried forward to the next year. There is no state or university-wise ceiling. (Source: `nfst_part_a_full_guidelines_2021-26.pdf`)
* **NFST-S-02**: Slots are distributed using a priority cascade: (1) Divyangjan — 38 slots (5% of 750), (2) PVTG — 25 slots, (3) Female — 225 slots (30% of 750, overlapping with female Divyangjan/PVTG), (4) ST Others — 462 slots. Unfilled slots in a higher-priority bucket cascade down. (Source: `nfst_part_a_full_guidelines_2021-26.pdf`)
* **NFST-S-03**: Candidates holding offers from IIT/AIIMS/IIM/IISER are given priority. The 'ST Others' bucket is reduced proportionately to accommodate them. (Source: `nfst_part_a_full_guidelines_2021-26.pdf`)

### NOS
* **NOS-S-01**: The scheme awards 20 total slots per year, distributed as 17 for ST and 3 for PVTG. (Source: `RevisedGuidelinesNOS07102022.pdf` / `nos_faq_2025-26.pdf.pdf`)
* **NOS-S-02**: 30% of the awards are earmarked for female candidates. (Source: `RevisedGuidelinesNOS07102022.pdf` / `nos_faq_2025-26.pdf.pdf`)

## Scoring / Merit

### NFST
* **NFST-M-01**: Under base guidelines, merit is based on PG examination marks. (Source: `nfst_part_a_full_guidelines_2021-26.pdf`, Section 3.5)
* **NFST-M-02**: For the 2025-26 cycle, scoring is 50% weightage to UGC-NET/CSIR-NET score and 50% weightage to Master's (or 4-year Bachelor's with Research) marks, both scaled to 100. (Source: `Notice-RegardingSelectionofMeritList-2025-26.pdf`)
* **NFST-M-03**: Method for scaling/normalizing NET scores and handling candidates without a NET score must be configurable. (Source: Unspecified/Pending, marked verified:false)

### NOS
* **NOS-M-01**: First priority is given to candidates already admitted to top 1,000 QS ranked foreign institutes. (Source: `RevisedGuidelinesNOS07102022.pdf`)
* **NOS-M-02**: Ties are broken based on marks/grades secured in the qualifying examination. (Source: `RevisedGuidelinesNOS07102022.pdf`)

## Lifecycle Stages

### NFST
* **NFST-L-01**: Application phase on portal opens around 1 July and closes around 30 September. (Source: `nfst_part_a_full_guidelines_2021-26.pdf`)
* **NFST-L-02**: Applications undergo institute-level document verification, followed by Ministry-level verification. Defective applications are returned for resubmission. (Source: `nfst_part_a_full_guidelines_2021-26.pdf`)
* **NFST-L-03**: Merit list is prepared by a Selection Committee chaired by the Secretary MoTA. (Source: `nfst_part_a_full_guidelines_2021-26.pdf`)
* **NFST-L-04**: Following provisional merit list and award letter issuance, scholar must submit a joining report within 1 month, or the award is cancelled and offered to a waitlisted candidate. (Source: `nfst_part_a_full_guidelines_2021-26.pdf`)
* **NFST-L-05**: Scholar must submit quarterly continuation certificates (due 10 Jul / 10 Oct / 10 Jan / 10 Apr), a yearly progress report, and eventually a thesis upload to gate final payment and course completion certificate. (Source: `nfst_part_a_full_guidelines_2021-26.pdf`)

### NOS
* **NOS-L-01**: Lifecycle follows application, provisional award, verification by the Indian Embassy, confirmation award, and ongoing progress reports. (Source: `RevisedGuidelinesNOS07102022.pdf`)

## Rates / Amounts

### NFST
* **NFST-R-01**: M.Phil fellowship rate: ₹31,000/month (effective 1 April 2022). (Source: `nfst_part_a_full_guidelines_2021-26.pdf`)
* **NFST-R-02**: Ph.D fellowship rate: ₹31,000/month for first 2 years, ₹35,000/month for remaining 3 years. (Source: `nfst_part_a_full_guidelines_2021-26.pdf`)
* **NFST-R-03**: Contingency grant: M.Phil is ₹10,000/year (Humanities) or ₹12,000/year (Science/Engg). Ph.D is ₹20,500/year (Humanities) or ₹25,000/year (Science/Engg). (Source: `nfst_part_a_full_guidelines_2021-26.pdf`)
* **NFST-R-04**: HRA is provided at UGC rates (8%/16%/24% by city category). (Source: `nfst_part_a_full_guidelines_2021-26.pdf`)
* **NFST-R-05**: Escort allowance for Divyangjan is ₹2,000/month. (Source: `nfst_part_a_full_guidelines_2021-26.pdf`)
* **NFST-R-06**: Current exact rates for the current year. (Source: Pending "Revised rate of fellowship w.e.f 1.1.2023", verified:false)

### NOS
* **NOS-R-01**: Scheme covers tuition fees, visa fees, and air passage. (Source: `RevisedGuidelinesNOS07102022.pdf`)
* **NOS-R-02**: Annual maintenance allowance is $15,400 in the US or £9,900 in the UK. (Source: `RevisedGuidelinesNOS07102022.pdf`)
* **NOS-R-03**: An annual contingency allowance is provided. (Source: `RevisedGuidelinesNOS07102022.pdf`)

## Post-Selection Rules

### NFST
* **NFST-P-01**: Tenure is 2 years for M.Phil, and 5 years for M.Phil+Ph.D or Ph.D. Continuation from M.Phil to Ph.D does not require a fresh application (max 5 years total). (Source: `nfst_part_a_full_guidelines_2021-26.pdf`, Section 5.4.2)
* **NFST-P-02**: Leave policy allows 30 days/year plus public holidays; maternity/paternity leave per Govt norms extending the fellowship; "intermittent break" up to 1 year total (max 3 uses) for women scholars; academic leave without fellowship up to 1 year once. (Source: `nfst_part_a_full_guidelines_2021-26.pdf`)
* **NFST-P-03**: University transfer and subject/course changes are permitted with prior institute approval. (Source: `nfst_part_a_full_guidelines_2021-26.pdf`)
* **NFST-P-04**: Discontinuation requires a submitted undertaking; refund obligations apply if scholarship was availed elsewhere. Cancellation applies on proven misconduct or subsequent ineligibility. (Source: `nfst_part_a_full_guidelines_2021-26.pdf`)

### NOS
* **NOS-P-01**: Course and institute changes are restricted. (Source: `RevisedGuidelinesNOS07102022.pdf`)
* **NOS-P-02**: Mandatory 6-monthly progress reports must be submitted. (Source: `RevisedGuidelinesNOS07102022.pdf`)
* **NOS-P-03**: Award is cancelled with conditions if the student returns to India without completing the course. (Source: `RevisedGuidelinesNOS07102022.pdf`)
